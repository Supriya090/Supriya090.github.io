Hello, I am Supriya!


I am a PhD student in Information Technology at George Mason University, where I am advised by Dr. Sanchari Das in the Data Agency & Security (DAS) Lab. My research investigates usable privacy and security for AI agents and companions, with a current focus on systems that handle sensitive health information, alongside broader HCI work on trust, authentication, and user control across AI and emerging technologies.

I was also a Visiting Researcher in the CHAI Lab at Northeastern University, working with Dr. Smit Desai on empathy in conversational agents. Prior to my PhD, I completed my Master of Science by Research (MRes) in Artificial Intelligence at Coventry University, UK, supervised by Dr. Xiaorui Jiang and Prof. Vasile Palade. My thesis, "Automated Clinical Coding with a Hybrid Evidence-Based Pipeline: Investigating Dataset Quality, Undercoding and Coding Errors," focused on optimising the clinical coding process using large and pre-trained language models, and was supported by the British Council Women in STEM Scholarship.

My technical foundation was built at the Institute of Engineering, Pulchowk Campus (Tribhuvan University) in Nepal, where I completed my Bachelor's in Computer Engineering. It was there, amidst a vibrant community of brilliant minds, that I first experimented with machine learning and discovered the potential of technology to solve real-world problems.

When I'm Away from the Keyboard

I love going on hikes, even though my body gives up almost instantly when climbing uphill! I also love buying novels, reading novels, and talking about novels in my free time. If you ask me for recommendations, these are probably the ones I will suggest. The list includes every genre; take your pick!

I also enjoy watching live theatre, movies, and series. If you want to talk about NLP, books, or movies, hit me up!
